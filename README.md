AI Operations Intelligence

«Transform Google Play Store reviews into executive-level operational intelligence using Python, NLP, LLMs, and Streamlit.»

Executive Overview

AI Operations Intelligence is an end-to-end AI platform that automatically collects customer reviews from the Google Play Store, classifies operational issues using NLP, identifies recurring business themes with Large Language Models (LLMs), and generates executive-level recommendations through an interactive Streamlit dashboard.

The objective is simple: turn raw customer feedback into actionable operational decisions.
